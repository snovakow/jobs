CLOSING NOTES, RUN 1
Patterns. Seg1: Canadian openings split between browser 3D and data viewers (Autodesk, NearSource, the TalentFish client) and rendering teams built on Gaussian splatting or neural rendering (Waabi, Miris) that pair graphics with ML, C++ or Python requirements; native graphics roles (Mozilla, SideFX, the TalentBurst client) are gated on C++ or Rust. Seg3: roles name a rendering, charting or collaboration surface in TypeScript and React (Grafana's visualization team, Pantheon's collaborative editing platform, Caddie's SVG and Canvas work, NearSource's WebGL viewers), mostly at staff or principal level with leadership and on-call duties. Seg2: the Canadian openings are real-time transport and stream-compositing roles gated on C++, Rust or Go (Mecka, Xsolla). Seg4: video-product iOS roles (Tubi, TribalScale, the GuruLink client) ask for current Swift and SwiftUI with tvOS and AVFoundation playback depth, not Objective-C.

Adjacent shapes worth a second look: staffing-firm and consultancy contract postings that list WebGL or 3D in a skills block (TalentFish, NearSource, TalentBurst); 3D asset-pipeline and integration engineering at 3D platform companies (Miris); full-stack architecture roles on collaborative-editing and viewer platforms (Pantheon, Autodesk) titled for the platform rather than for graphics; consultancy iOS and tvOS roles whose client product is a streaming app (TribalScale); and automotive simulation tooling that is web graphics under a full-stack title (GM).
