CLOSING NOTES, RUN 1

Patterns by segment
- Seg1 (Graphics/XR): the live Canadian graphics reqs are low-level engine work: browser and engine internals in C++ and Rust, and graphics-API abstraction layers across Vulkan, D3D12 and Metal. Unity's Montreal Web Graphics reqs are the exception that fits a web-graphics resume: "Graphics Developer, Web Graphics" ($96,200–$144,200 per Indeed CA) and "Senior Graphics Developer, Web Graphics" ($110,200–$168,900), both stating "We enable beautiful realtime 3D graphics in web browsers via WebGL and WebGPU". This run saw them only in search snippets and could not open them on Unity's Workday board, so they have no rows. C++ is a constant in these reqs, though Unity's Kernel API req says "Graphics fundamentals matter more to us here than experience with any one API."
- Seg3 (Senior frontend): the depth roles are data visualization and canvas rendering, and they are senior or staff, remote, and open to Canada. Both postings found name TypeScript and React as table stakes and judge candidates on rendering performance.
- Seg2 (Video infrastructure): the player work at streaming companies has moved onto native clients. Tubi's Toronto board now carries iOS rather than web-player reqs, and its WebOTT req sits in San Francisco.
- Seg4 (Apple platforms): video-product iOS roles in Ontario all want current Swift/SwiftUI and AVFoundation depth. Tubi's req adds visionOS, which suits a 360° and stereoscopic video background more than most.

Adjacent role shapes worth not dismissing
- Browser-engine graphics maintenance contracts: they look like pure C++ jobs, but the domain is WebGL, canvas and compositing, and an engineer who has hit those bugs from the content side brings real diagnostic value.
- Staff-level data-viz panel work on open-source observability tools: charting is visualization, and your heat-map and analytics work counts as domain evidence.
- Native video apps that span iOS, tvOS and visionOS: these teams value spatial and immersive video experience that most iOS candidates lack.

Where each segment came up dry, and why
- Seg1: CoLab's only 3D engineering req is backend geometry and ML analysis, not rendering, so it failed the test. Unity's live graphics reqs that could be opened are either the Montreal "Graphics Engineer, Kernel API Team" (JOBREQ-2616449, "CAD$89,100 - CAD$133,700"), a C++ abstraction layer over "D3D12, Vulkan, Metal, WebGPU and console backends" that is not strong enough to clear the RELOCATE bar, or a US-zoned PolySpatial role with no visa sponsorship that requires Unity plus another high-end engine's rendering architecture. SideFX's careers page rendered no job list without JavaScript. The search budget ran out before Google and the rest of the list.
- Seg3: BioRender's Gem board would not render its listings, so its strongest-fit req could only be read on a republisher. Grafana's other Canada roles that came up are full-stack observability roles that were not opened.
- Seg2: Stream's search results surfaced only backend, moderation and infrastructure roles in Toronto, and no Ashby board page came up that could be opened. Tubi's Toronto board has no video-player or WebOTT req; its WebOTT role is in San Francisco and was not opened. Mozilla's web-conferencing front-end contract returned 404 and is no longer on its listings page.
- Seg4: Warner Bros. Discovery's Kanata Senior Software Developer - tvOS req returned 410 (gone). The search budget ran out before Quickplay, Clarius, Apple and the media companies.
