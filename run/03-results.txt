Patterns by segment
- seg1: Canadian graphics reqs fall into three shapes:
  - C++/Rust engine, browser or XR-system work. Even the web-graphics and XR
    interface roles gate on C++.
  - Python/C++ neural-rendering and 3DGS asset pipelines at autonomy
    companies.
  - A thin band of TypeScript plus Three.js/WebXR web roles, which is where
    you are strongest.
  Gaussian splatting now shows up as pipeline infrastructure more than as a
  renderer to build.
- seg3: Depth appears in three places: canvas and timeline editors,
  data-visualization frontends, and browser tooling at autonomy, robotics and
  drone companies. Most Toronto "senior frontend" listings are
  marketing-site, experimentation or CRUD work. Staff-level postings now ask
  for AI-assisted development fluency, which your agent-led TypeScript port
  already demonstrates.
- seg2: OTT player engineering in Canada is native (iOS/tvOS/Android) or
  C++/Rust, and broadcast-vendor software reqs are C/C++ codec work. The
  web-side media openings came through browser media capture, and through
  real-time video inside operations apps that seg3 searches found.
- seg4: The Apple-platform reqs at Toronto and Montreal streamers were senior
  native iOS/tvOS roles asking for 7+ years of Swift/SwiftUI, which is the
  shape your brief excludes. Apple's own Canadian board had no
  visualization-first engineering reqs.

Adjacent shapes you'd likely skip but shouldn't
- Browser tooling at autonomy, robotics and drone companies: flight decks,
  simulation viewers and labelling UIs are 3D and video web visualization
  under "tools" or "frontend" titles.
- Founding frontend roles at AI-media startups building editors with canvas,
  timeline and node graphs. These map to visual scripting plus multimedia.
- Observability and data-viz frontends. They are 2D, but visualization is the
  core of the job, and they are friendly to remote Canada.
- Fixed-term contracts at a browser vendor that hires Remote Canada, in
  graphics or media capture.
- Developer-integration and SDK roles at 3D-delivery startups that accept
  TypeScript.
