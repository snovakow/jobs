Patterns and gaps in this run:

Of the 18 named target companies, only three (Geotab, Kinaxis, Esri Canada) currently have a live, verifiable posting that clears the technical-depth bar. The other two rows came from staffing-agency postings for undisclosed clients, not any of the 18 companies directly - the purest 3D/XR matches in this whole run were anonymized contract placements, not permanent roles at named employers.

A recurring, useful-but-easy-to-miss shape: "video frontend" work at IoT/telematics companies (Geotab) and node/graph-diagram visualization at enterprise B2B SaaS (Kinaxis) don't read as "graphics jobs" from the title, but the day-to-day overlaps your immersive-3D and visual-scripting skill set more than a generic "frontend engineer" posting would suggest. Worth broadening searches to "frontend + visualization" or "frontend + video" at plain enterprise software companies, not just creative/3D-native studios.

Where it came up dry, and why: Shopify, Autodesk, Mappedin, Ecopia AI, Vidyard, Konrad, Jam3, SideFX, Maplesoft, Ansys, Miovision, Coveo, Lightspeed, Wealthsimple, and PagerDuty had no current frontend role clearing the technical-depth test at time of search - either the only matching req had closed in the last few months (Autodesk's 3D role, both Mappedin dev roles, both Coveo frontend roles, PagerDuty's frontend role all showed explicit "removed" notices when opened), the live postings were junior/generic full-stack without canvas/WebGL/viz/real-time/node-editor depth (Konrad, Ecopia, Ansys, Lightspeed), or nothing relevant was posted at all (Shopify, Vidyard, Jam3, SideFX, Maplesoft, Miovision, Wealthsimple). I did not get to widen the search meaningfully beyond the named list and a couple of ad-hoc keyword searches, so there's real upside left in a broader Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby sweep for Toronto/Waterloo/Ottawa "senior frontend" + Three.js/WebGL/data-viz roles outside this specific company list.
