NORMALIZATION APPLIED (UNCHANGED FROM LAST PASS)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role key: case-folded; dashes and slashes read as spaces; remaining punctuation
dropped; repeated spaces collapsed; a trailing location or remote/hybrid/on-
site suffix dropped. Company key: the same, plus trailing legal and decorative
suffixes dropped - inc, ltd, limited, corp, corporation, llc, lp, co, company,
studio, studios, technologies, technology, group, ai, plc, gmbh. Stored role
titles keep the posting's own wording minus any trailing location or remote
suffix.

Two reconciliation rules were needed this pass and are recorded here so the
next pass matches the same way:

  - COMPANY PREFIX. Where one side's normalized company is a word-prefix of the
    other's AND the role keys are identical, they are one company. This
    resolved "Evertz" (seg2) against "Evertz Microsystems" (ledger) and
    "Mozilla (Thunderbird)" (seg4) against "Mozilla" (ledger). The identical-
    role-key condition is what keeps it safe: "Esri Inc." and "Esri Canada" are
    both prefixed by "esri" but their role keys differ, so they stayed separate
    companies, which is correct.
  - SOURCE-ATTRIBUTABLE WORDING. Where a matched posting's location wording
    differs only because this pass read a different board than last pass, and
    the candidate-facing facts (country, remote eligibility, city) are
    unchanged, that is not a material change. The rule is that the posting must
    have changed, not our reading of it.
