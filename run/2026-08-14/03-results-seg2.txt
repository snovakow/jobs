Then, briefly:

Pattern in what this segment is actually asking for. Almost every posting I could verify split cleanly into two buckets: deep C/C++/Go backend work on encoding, transcoding, or distributed video infrastructure (Evertz, Mux, Dejero's live listings when they existed), or browser/app-layer player and delivery work bundled inside a general "Software Developer" req at a broadcaster or streamer (Bell Media's recurring "Video Pipeline, Bell MediaTV" family, CBC's Web Platform postings, Vimeo's Video Pro role). Your resume sits almost entirely in the second bucket — which is the rarer, faster-cycling one; several of these closed within the days between being indexed and my checking them.

Adjacent roles you might have dismissed. "Software Developer" or "Full Stack Developer" titles at broadcasters and streaming-adjacent companies (Bell Media, CBC, Rogers) routinely turn out to be the video-pipeline or player team once you open the description — the title alone gives no signal. Betting/sports-streaming companies (Bally's Interactive's Bally Sports Live team) are also quietly running real video-player engineering orgs under gaming-industry branding, worth watching even though nothing was open there this pass.

Where the search came up dry, and why. Ross Video, Vidyard, Brightcove, Daily.co, and Haivision each had zero open engineering roles on their own boards — not a filtering artifact, their career pages are just currently empty of SWE reqs. Rogers, Corus, Telus Digital, Cisco/Webex, Google/YouTube (Waterloo/Toronto), Grass Valley, and Enghouse/Vidyo turned up no roles that were both open and video-pipeline-specific despite direct board checks. Bell Media and CBC/Radio-Canada are clearly the most active posters for this exact profile in Ontario — both had multiple strong-match postings that had just closed when I checked; given how often they repost, their boards are worth a direct look again in the next week or two rather than relying on aggregator caches.
