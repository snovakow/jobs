5.2  "LEAD WITH META QUEST, THE POLY AWARDS, AND FRAUNHOFER HHI OMAF"
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the one that should shift, and this pass gives a sharper answer than
the positioning brief could, because it can point at the actual postings.

Move all three below the fold for Tier 1 entries 2, 3 and 4. Three of your
four Tier 1 roles are enterprise 3D platform and tooling work — Autodesk's
viewer platform, Waabi's internal simulation tooling, Ecopia's visualization
engine. To those readers, a consumer-XR headline says "specialist in
something I am not hiring for", and the awards in particular read as filler
because an unfamiliar award with a superlative name is indistinguishable from
an invented one.

What replaces the lead in all three cases: you wrote the rendering library
this product ran on, then migrated the live product off it onto Three.js.
That is two hard things and a judgement call, it is dated to your current
job, and it survives contact with any of the three readers.

Keep the framing intact for exactly one Tier 1 role. Entry 1, Autodesk's
Senior Frontend Developer (3D), names WebXR for AR and VR in the requirements
and asks for a portfolio of 3D web projects. Meta Quest and the Poly Awards
are portfolio evidence there, in support of your third bullet. Not the
headline, but they belong on the page.

Keep Fraunhofer and OMAF for Tier 2 entries 6 and 7 — Figma's video rendering
and playback role and Vimeo's video-tools role — where standards-level video
work is a genuine differentiator and the audience recognizes it. Cut it
entirely from Ecopia. It is noise to a geospatial team.

roles are at companies whose own postings are dated within the last two
weeks. The gap between those two facts is what a reviewer notices. Nothing in
this document fixes it except item 4's WebGPU port, which is why that

technology. Autodesk Principal turns on whether you can show judgement at
platform scale, and Waabi Web Tools turns on whether a frontend specialist

skills lists, and you have the stories. Have the migration one polished cold
before you send either application.
