Patterns and dry wells, briefly:

The live openings at target companies split into two shapes: native/console-engine graphics work requiring strong C++ (Rockstar's Graphics Programmer, Digital Extremes' Senior Graphics Programmer before it closed, BlackBerry QNX's GPU-driver roles, Ansys's GPU R&D role) — a poor match given weak C++ — versus roles at data, mapping, or simulation companies embedding WebGL/Three.js into a browser-based visualization or digital-twin product (Ecopia, Waabi, Autodesk) — a much closer fit to the Liquid Cinema/Zygote arc of the resume.

The adjacent shape worth watching for: plain "Frontend Engineer" or "Full-Stack Developer" titles at mapping/geospatial/simulation companies that never say "3D" or "graphics" in the title but do real-time WebGL work in the body. Ecopia's listing is exactly this — title alone would have filtered it out, which is presumably why the brief insisted on reading past the title.

Where it came up dry: SideFX, Rockstar, Ubisoft, Digital Extremes (beyond the general web-eng role), BlackBerry QNX, MDA Space, Synaptive Medical, Structura Biotechnology, Esri Canada, Geotab, Teledyne Optech, Klick, Konrad, Meta, Google, Unity Technologies, Cesium, Felix and Paul Studios, Thales Canada, and Jam3 (now absorbed into Monks/S4Capital, no Toronto graphics opening) — no live, verifiable posting cleared the segment's technical-depth test at time of search, either because nothing was currently open, the only open roles were unrelated (sales, IT support, embedded firmware), or a promising-looking listing turned out to be closed when checked directly against the employer's own board. Broad keyword sweeps (WebGL, Three.js, WebXR, react-three-fiber) across Wellfound, Built In Toronto, and general Canadian boards did not turn up additional postings that both matched the segment and could be verified live.
