===============================================================================
STEP 5 — APPLY
===============================================================================

Tier 1 only. Use the three lead bullets and the opening line the triage gave
you for each posting.

Set each one's status to applied in ledger.csv as you go, not later. If you
skip this, the next pass resurfaces the same roles and re-ranks them, and the
ledger becomes noise.

Roles you consciously pass on go to skipped. Anything left at new will
correctly resurface next pass — that is the ledger working, not failing.

Then clear the tier 3 queue, this pass and not later. Triage writes every row
as new, tier 3 included, so tier 3 lands in your queue rather than filed away.
Sort the new rows by tier, read the five-word reasons in notes, and set the
ones you agree with to skipped. Two minutes while the postings are still
fresh. If one is worth applying to: apply, set it to applied, leave tier at 3,
and add to notes what triage undervalued.

When a rejection arrives, set that row to rejected — from the inbox, the day
it lands. Silence is not a rejection; leave those at applied.

Rows sitting at new past their window are not clutter to clear out. They are
the pile you did not get to. Apply, or set them to skipped.

The four statuses, for when you are editing the file:

  new         triage wrote it; nothing decided yet
  applied     you sent it
  rejected    they said no
  skipped     you passed

new is the only one triage writes, and the only one that lets a posting back
into next pass's ranking.
