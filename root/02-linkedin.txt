CHECK EACH SEARCH — before reading a search's results, confirm that the
keywords parameter in the address is exactly the description and that Past

check fails, fix the search, run it again, and record nothing from the
search that went wrong.

SOURCE CODES — every row names the description that captured it: li1,
li1b, li2, li3 or li4, after the description's label.

WHAT TO READ — every result on page 1, in order, including any below a line
saying further results "may not be exact matches". When nothing is an exact
match, a notice above the first result says instead that LinkedIn had
trouble finding exact matches. Say in the coverage list where the line fell,
or that the notice appeared, or that neither did. Capture a result whose
title plainly names the description's kind of work. A title that names only
a role, such as frontend or full-stack engineer, does not: treat it as
ambiguous. Open a result whose title is ambiguous but plausibly on-topic,
and capture or drop it on what the posting says. A Promoted label changes
nothing.

HOW MUCH TO OPEN — never open a result whose title is plainly unrelated,
Remote or not. Open every result you capture whose location says Remote,
since eligibility clauses hide in the body of the posting, and take its
location wording from the posting. Take every other result you capture from
its card, unless the card's location wording or its title is ambiguous.

LINKS — each row's URL is https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/<id>/, with
<id> the posting's id. A result card carries no such link: read its id from
the page code, the number in the card's job-card-component-ref attribute,
rather than clicking the card for it.

STATUS TOKEN — begin the location column of every row with one of these,
followed by an em dash and then the posting's own wording:

  RELOCATE    Stated location is outside Ontario, or outside Canada.
  UNCLEAR     Remote, with no country stated.

Do not skip US-only postings or postings requiring US work authorization.

Token it and move on. It changes nothing about what you open. On a Remote
result, capturing them costs nothing. You have already spent the open by the
time you learn a posting is US-only, so recording it is free where discarding

This token is a mechanical read of what the posting states. Do not judge
