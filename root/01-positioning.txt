INDEED RULES, AS APPLIED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each keyword is a phrase real postings use and names a technology, a standard,
a kind of work or a title that names the work; none is a generic title, an
ordinary English word with a large non-technical job market, or a phrase whose
market is mostly analysts'. Rejected on those rules: Metal (the canonical
trap), canvas (Canvas LMS and art), rendering (print, architecture), streaming
(music, gaming), simulation (training in every field), maps (retail,
logistics), video (every media job), immersive (hospitality, marketing), data
visualization (business intelligence: 13 of the 14 set 3 rows in the pass of
2026-10-18 were analyst, data science or BI seats, as were nine rows the pass
before, which is why HTML5 canvas replaced it), HLS (health and life sciences
consulting), OTT and connected TV (advertising sales and media buying), Swift
(an ordinary word and a banking network), SwiftUI and Objective-C (they aim at
native app work for its own sake), and Apple TV (a Toronto pass already
returned an Apple TV business manager seat).

Three.js and real-time collaboration were kept: Indeed tokenizes the period and
the hyphen away and the phrase still matches the way postings write it.
Gaussian splatting is left out because a 14-day Indeed window in Canada almost
never carries it; it stays in the sweep keywords and in LinkedIn description 1.

Set 4 has found nothing in either of its two passes, 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-18:
no ledger row carries in4t or in4r. It is kept as it stands. Its four keywords
name exactly the native video and 3D work segment 4 is for. Every broader Apple
keyword is a trap or aims at native work with no video in it, and the native
video roles that did reach Indeed came through set 2's Toronto pass, which
found Ring's Builder - Mobile (Platform). A set that returns nothing for the
work you want is the market, which is what the playbook says to expect of
Indeed.

  - Scale and process. Nothing on the resume shows work inside a larger

    trains, on-call, design documents. A hiring manager at a 200-plus-person
    company will assume you have not done it, and the resume gives them nothing
    to argue with. This, more than any skill, is the likeliest reason a strong
    fit comes back a no.
  - Levelling and pay. Senior Developer, Frontend Lead at a company whose size
    the resume never states maps to Senior, not Staff, at most large companies;
